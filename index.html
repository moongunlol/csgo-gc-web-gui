AWP Inventory Forge
Build your CS:GO weapon inventory in minutes
Pick any weapon skin, knife, glove, agent, sticker or music kit — then download a ready-to-use inventory.txt file.
How It Works
1
Get the skin database
Download all.json from ByMykel/CSGO-API — it's a free file that contains all weapon skins, stickers & music kits.
2
Load all.json
Click Load all.json below and pick the file you downloaded. Nothing leaves your computer — it all runs locally.
3
Pick a weapon
In Choose Your Items (left panel), pick a category like Rifles, then click the weapon you want.
4
Choose a skin
Click any skin card in the middle panel. Optionally adjust the float and pattern seed on the left.
5
Add to queue
Click + Add to Queue. Repeat for every skin you want — stickers and music kits too.
6
Generate & save
Click ⚙ Generate File, then ⬇ Download inventory.txt. Drop it into your csgo_gc folder replacing the old file.
