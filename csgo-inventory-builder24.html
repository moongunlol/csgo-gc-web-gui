Quick Start Guide
1
Get the item database
Download all.json from ByMykel/CSGO-API — it contains all weapon skins, agents & music kits.
2
Load all.json
Click the button below to load the file locally. Nothing is uploaded — it all runs in your browser.
3
Pick a weapon
In Item Config (left panel) choose a category and weapon. The skin browser auto-filters to match.
4
Choose a skin
Click any skin card in the Skin Browser (middle). Set float, pattern, StatTrak in Item Config.
5
Add to queue
Click + Add to Queue. Repeat for every item you want. Use Multi-pick for bulk adding.
6
Generate & save
Click ⚙ Generate, then Download to save as inventory.txt. Drop it into your csgo_gc folder, replacing the existing one.
